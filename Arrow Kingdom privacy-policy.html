📱 Device Permissions Used by Arrow Kingdom

To ensure an immersive and smooth casual gaming experience, the Application requests or utilizes the following permissions on Your device:

🔊 Audio Playback & Sound Effects

Allows the game to play background music (ambient kingdom melodies) and interactive sound effects (arrow release, target hit, level victory fanfare, and button taps). The game only outputs audio and does not record audio or access your microphone.

📳 Haptic & Vibration Feedback (android.permission.VIBRATE)

Enables brief, gentle haptic tactile vibrations when interacting with game elements, such as clearing arrows, unlocking chests, or tapping buttons. Users can toggle haptic feedback on or off at any time inside the in-game Settings menu.

💾 Local Device Storage & Offline Game Saves

Arrow Kingdom is designed as a privacy-friendly, 100% offline-playable game. Your progress, unlocked worlds, themes, high scores, coins, gems, and player preferences are securely saved locally on your device via AsyncStorage. We do not upload your gameplay progress to external personal tracking servers.

🌐 Network & Internet Access (Diagnostic Only)

Used solely for standard application updates, Google Play services verification, and crash reporting diagnostics. We do not track or sell your browsing history or personal identification.

🛡️ Family-Friendly & Offline First: Arrow Kingdom does not require account creation, does not access device contacts, camera, or precise GPS location, and does not sell user data to third parties.
