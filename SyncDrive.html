Critical Permissions & Telemetry Data Collection

To provide advanced driving analytics, scoring engines, and profile personalization features, SyncDrive requests access to sensitive device permissions. We collect and process the following telemetry data with your prior permission:

Location Tracking Motion Sensors Audio/Microphone Camera Access Photo Library

A. Detailed Location Data (Foreground Tracking)

While using the Application to track a trip, we collect precise latitude, longitude, speed, and heading coordinates from your device's GPS system.

  • Purpose: We use location coordinates to map your driving route, display route replays on the map, and measure vehicle speed to generate safety metrics.
  • Data Handling: This telemetry data is gathered exclusively in the foreground while a driving tracking session is active. Location traces are processed locally and are not transmitted to third-party servers without your explicit direction.

B. Motion and Sensor Telemetry

We read high-frequency data from your device's physical hardware sensors (including the **Accelerometer**, **Gyroscope**, and **Device Motion** sensors).

  • Purpose: This data is processed through a Low-Pass Filter (LPF) to identify rapid decelerations (Harsh Braking), quick accelerations (Harsh Acceleration), and heavy lateral forces (Aggressive Cornering/Sharp Turns).
  • Data Handling: All raw sensor telemetry remains local to your device and is used dynamically during active tracking sessions.

C. Audio and Microphone Access (RECORD_AUDIO)

SyncDrive requests access to your device's microphone via the android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO permission.

  • Purpose: Microphone access is used to enable voice-based AI driving commands, driver voice alerts, or real-time hands-free coaching triggers.
  • Data Handling: Audio recording only occurs when you trigger a voice feature. Audio data is processed strictly locally for speech processing and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted to any external servers without your explicit commands.

D. Pictures and Media Access (Camera and Gallery)

SyncDrive requests access to your device's camera and photo gallery to capture or select a profile picture.

  • Purpose: Allows you to upload or capture a profile photo to personalize your driver profile.
  • Data Handling: Photos are stored strictly on your device's local memory store (MMKV) and are used solely for UI rendering.

🔒 Data Privacy & Local Processing Principle: SyncDrive prioritizes offline privacy. Telemetry calculation, safety scoring, events analysis, and AI safety coach insights are processed locally on your physical device using secure MMKV storage. No location traces or voice snippets are sold or distributed.
