2. Android Device Permissions Requested

To provide specific interactive features of the Application, PollSphere requests access to certain hardware and system capabilities on Your device with Your prior explicit consent:

📷 Camera Access (android.permission.CAMERA)
Allows You to scan QR codes to instantly discover and join live polling rooms. Camera data is processed locally in real-time solely for QR recognition.
🖼️ Photo Library & Storage (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE & WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE)
Allows You to save generated poll room QR cards and live result summary graphics directly to Your device's gallery, or select QR images from your photo library.
🎙️ Audio Recording (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO)
Requested for optional real-time audio interaction features within live poll rooms when supported.

You can grant or revoke these permissions at any time through Your mobile device settings.
